Threat Intelligence: Remotely Exploitable Medical Devices

Final Project · OSINT Analyst Program (InsAIghts Academy by Penlink, with Google & Reichman) · April 2026

A decade-long (2015–2025) threat intelligence investigation into life-critical connected devices (pacemakers, insulin pumps, patient monitors and infusion systems) across 5 manufacturers, using 7 OSINT research layers plus a threat-actor track.

  • Every device category examined was found remotely exploitable, with CVSS scores up to 9.3–10.0
  • Vendor responses ranged from proactive (J&J warned 114,000 patients) to negligent (Contec: no response for 14+ months after a CISA/FDA backdoor advisory)
  • Investigated Contec CMS8000's hard-coded IP: WHOIS/DNS, Wayback history and geolocation to a university network in Wuhan
  • Delivered an entity network, risk matrix, threat-actor matrix and recommendations for hospitals, vendors and regulators
